# -Healthcare-Billing-Cost-Analysis-Dashboard-Power-BI-
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing healthcare billing and cost data for a medical facility, built to track revenue, costs, profitability, and provider (doctor) performance across departments and services.

Project Goals
Analyze total treatment cost vs. revenue to calculate profit margin
Track insurance coverage vs. out-of-pocket/patient-paid amounts
Identify the most costly departments, diagnoses, and procedures
Monitor payment status (Paid vs. Pending) to improve collections
Evaluate each doctor's performance in terms of patient count, revenue, and profitability

📊 Dashboard Contents

1-Main Page

Total treatment cost, medication cost, room cost, insurance coverage, total billing amount, profit, and total patients
Billing breakdown by: department, diagnosis, procedure, payment status, referral source, service type, room type
Billing trend over time (year/quarter/month)

2-Provider Page

Total billing, patient count, and profit by doctor
Patient distribution by doctor and department
Patient distribution by doctor and procedure

3-More Details Page

A Key Insights summary highlighting the main findings and takeaways from the data

🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI (DAX, Data Modeling, Interactive Visuals)
Power Query for data cleaning and preparation

💡 Key Insights
Overall profit margin of roughly 56%
60.79% of billing is still "Pending" — a clear opportunity to improve collections
Cardiology and Orthopedics lead both revenue and cost
X-Ray and CT Scan are the most billed procedures
Noticeable performance variation across the five doctors in revenue and profitability
